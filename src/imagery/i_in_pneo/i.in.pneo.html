DESCRIPTION

i.in.pneo preprocesses Pléiades-NEO multispectral imagery (6 bands: red, green, blue, NIR, red-edge, deep-blue). It supports the workflow from raw digital numbers (DN) to top-of-atmosphere (TOA) radiance and, optionally, to surface reflectance using either physically based or image-based atmospheric correction.
